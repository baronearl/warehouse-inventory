Core System Capabilities

Comprehensive warehouse stock management and intelligent replenishment

Warehouse Stock Catalog

Relational inventory mapping covering categories, vendors, storage locations, unit pricing, and automated SKU barcode generation.

Stock Operations & Audit

Stock intake (receiving), dispatch (sales), internal transfers, and adjustment tracking with an immutable audit log.

Demand Forecasting Engine

Machine learning and time-series demand models (Moving Averages, Exponential Smoothing, Prophet) predicting upcoming reorder requirements.

Real-Time Stockout Prevention

Dynamic safety-stock calculation and automated purchase recommendations based on lead times and forecasted consumption.
